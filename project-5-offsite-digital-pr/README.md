# Project 5 — Offsite SEO & Digital PR Campaign Blueprint

## Executive Summary

This project designs a digital PR and offsite SEO campaign for a B2B SaaS data integration platform. The campaign is built to earn authoritative links, brand mentions, and review-site authority that improve organic rankings and AI-search citations.

The blueprint includes:
- Linkable asset strategy
- Target publication and journalist list
- Outreach templates and cadence
- Reputation management plan
- KPI tracker
- PR-team partnership model

---

## 1. Campaign Objective

Position the B2B SaaS product as the best-in-class no-code ETL solution for mid-market data teams by earning high-authority links and brand mentions across data engineering, SaaS, and business-press outlets.

### Goals (12 Months)

| KPI | Baseline | Target |
|-----|----------|--------|
| Referring domains | 800 | 1,400 |
| Domain Rating (Ahrefs-equivalent) | 72 | 78 |
| Brand search volume trend | flat | +30% |
| Authority citations in AI answers | 18% | 35% |
| Review count on G2 | 270 | 500 |
| Referral sessions from PR/links | 4,000/mo | 10,000/mo |

## 2. Linkable Asset Strategy

### Asset 1: State of Data Integration Report
- **Format:** Annual report with original survey data.
- **Data:** 500+ data leaders surveyed on stack, budget, and tool preferences.
- **Earned media angle:** proprietary benchmarks and trend predictions.
- **Estimated links:** 40–80 high-authority links.

### Asset 2: ETL Pricing Calculator
- **Format:** Interactive tool comparing total cost of ownership across Fivetran, Airbyte, Stitch, Matillion, Hevo.
- **Earned media angle:** transparency in a famously opaque market.
- **Estimated links:** 30–50 links.

### Asset 3: Open Connector Quality Index
- **Format:** Public scorecard ranking connector reliability, latency, and coverage across major tools.
- **Earned media angle:** engineering credibility and vendor-neutral data.
- **Estimated links:** 25–40 links.

### Asset 4: Customer Data Story Series
- **Format:** 4 case studies with hard metrics (cost savings, hours saved, data volume).
- **Earned media angle:** real ROI proof points.
- **Estimated links:** 15–25 links.

## 6. Reputation Management Plan

### Review Sites
- G2: target 500 reviews, respond to every review within 48 hours.
- Capterra: maintain 4.5+ star average, update product details quarterly.
- Gartner Peer Insights: solicit enterprise customer reviews for analyst visibility.
- TrustRadius: encourage detailed reviews with screenshots and use cases.

### Comparison Pages
- Ensure accurate listings on SaaS comparison sites.
- Claim and update pages on AlternativeTo, SaaS Genius, Crozdesk.
- Provide fresh assets (logos, screenshots, feature lists) annually.

### Reddit / Community
- Monitor r/dataengineering, r/snowflake, r/analytics.
- Participate authentically; avoid astroturfing.
- AMA with head of product quarterly.
