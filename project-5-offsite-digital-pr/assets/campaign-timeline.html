12-Month Campaign Timeline

Quarter 1 — Foundation

Month 1

  • Finalize campaign goals and KPIs with leadership.
  • Build media list and review existing backlink profile.
  • Launch State of Data Integration survey.
  • Audit and claim review-site listings.

Month 2

  • Collect survey responses; begin report analysis.
  • Build PR kit: press release, executive quotes, data snippets.
  • Begin internal review of ETL pricing calculator concept.

Month 3

  • Publish State of Data Integration Report landing page.
  • Conduct embargo outreach to Tier 1/Tier 2 outlets.
  • Public launch + social amplification.
  • Track links and mentions.

Quarter 2 — Tools

Month 4

  • Launch ETL Pricing Calculator.
  • Outreach to SaaS pricing and community outlets.
  • Begin G2 review acquisition push.
  • Refresh review-site profiles with new assets.

Month 5

  • Publish first customer case study.
  • Outreach to vertical publications (retail, SaaS, fintech).
  • Launch internal link audit and reclaim broken links.

Month 6

  • Publish second case study.
  • Host Reddit AMA in r/dataengineering.
  • Mid-year KPI review and campaign adjustment.

Quarter 3 — Authority

Month 7

  • Launch Open Connector Quality Index.
  • Outreach to data engineering blogs and podcasts.
  • Pitch guest posts to Tier 2/3 outlets.

Month 8

  • Publish thought-leadership byline on data stack trends.
  • Secure podcast guest appearances.
  • Launch third case study.

Month 9

  • Run localized PR push in UK/DE/AU markets.
  • Optimize G2 category page for Q4 buying season.
  • Begin planning next year’s State of Data Integration report.

Quarter 4 — Scale

Month 10

  • Publish fourth case study.
  • Launch year-end "best of" data integration content.
  • Aggressive review acquisition for Q4.

Month 11

  • Repurpose report findings into blog series and social posts.
  • Pitch year-in-review stories to Tier 1 outlets.
  • Update all comparison pages and directories.

Month 12

  • Full-year campaign report.
  • Present results to leadership.
  • Plan next year’s asset calendar and budget.
